# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior, and verifiable outcomes.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes without personal attacks.
- Protect private information and never pressure others to disclose credentials or personal data.
- Respect project scope, licensing, and responsible security disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Insults, intimidation, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private source code, session data, or vulnerability details publicly.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or professionally inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy. They may issue warnings or temporarily or permanently restrict participation, depending on severity and history.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/AaAndrew233). Security vulnerabilities must use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.
